# First 12 Facebook Content Ideas

Post 2–3 times per week. Quality and consistency matter more than posting every day.

1. Introduction: who you are, what Alexion Studios does, and who you help.
2. Portfolio walkthrough: show a website or interface you built.
3. Quick tip: one simple website mistake that costs businesses customers.
4. Behind the scenes: your development workspace or work process.
5. Project story: the problem, your solution, and the technologies used.
6. Game-dev clip: share a prototype, mechanic, or learning moment.
7. Educational post: “What a professional website should include.”
8. Before/after: improve a sample landing page and explain why.
9. Personal founder post: why you started Alexion Studios.
10. FAQ: “How much does a website cost?” Explain that it depends on scope; invite messages for a quote.
11. Social media tip for a small business.
12. Clear offer: “I’m available for website and web-app projects. Message me with your idea.”

## Easy post format

1. Hook: name the problem or result.
2. Value: explain/show what you did.
3. Proof: screenshot, demo, code-free visual, or outcome.
4. Call to action: invite a message, comment, or portfolio visit.

## Weekly rhythm (draft)

- Tuesday: useful tip or educational post
- Thursday: project / behind-the-scenes post
- Saturday: offer, personal story, or portfolio proof
