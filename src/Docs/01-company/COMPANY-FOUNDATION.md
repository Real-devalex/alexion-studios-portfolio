## First target market (recommended starting point)

Start with **small service businesses and ambitious creators** that need a modern website. This is broad enough to find work but focused enough for clear marketing.

Examples: barbershops, salons, restaurants, photographers, fashion brands, event planners, tutors, gyms, small shops, musicians, and personal brands.

Why this is the best starting point:

- Your web-development skills are immediately useful to them.
- They understand the value of a better online presence.
- You can build realistic demos without needing a client first.
- A finished website is easy to show in Facebook posts and your portfolio.

Game development and social marketing remain Alexion Studios services, but they should be secondary offers until your web portfolio starts bringing inquiries.
